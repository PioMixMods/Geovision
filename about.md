# Geovision

**Geovision** is a customizable real-time music visualizer for Geometry Dash.

It reacts to the music playing in your level and lets you create your own visual style with a wide range of customization options.

## Features

- 🎵 **3 visualizer styles**
  - Bars
  - Wave
  - Radial
- 🎨 Custom Color 1 and Color 2
- 🌈 Optional color blending
- 🖼️ Match the visualizer to the level's background color
- 📐 Position, size and orientation controls
- ⚡ Power and sensitivity controls
- 🌊 Smoothing control
- 👁️ Opacity control
- 🚀 Performance optimization options
- 💾 Presets for saving and loading configurations
- 🔄 Supports music changes during gameplay

## Customization

Geovision is designed to let you build a visualizer that fits your level, your music, and your style.

Whether you want a simple spectrum, a smooth waveform, or a circular visualizer, you can customize how it looks and reacts to the music.
